The Neighbourhood

Four ways to spend a day

From a sunrise swim in a private cove to a late dinner above the bay — this is what the address actually buys you.

01 — Morning

A swim before the world wakes

Residents begin the day in heated salt-water pools oriented to the sunrise. For those who prefer the sea, three of the collection hold private beach access with a gated stair to the sand. A resident trainer can be arranged for a 6 a.m. session on the deck.

02 — Afternoon

Lunch, then nothing at all

Two Michelin-starred kitchens sit within a ten-minute drive, and our concierge holds standing tables at both. Prefer to stay in? An in-residence chef service can be arranged with a day's notice, pairing menus to the cellar already stocked in the house.

03 — Golden hour

Eighteen holes, then the terrace

Two private courses extend reciprocal privileges to Springs Estate residents, with tee times held each morning. The clubhouse terrace faces west; a late round finishes precisely as the light turns.

04 — Evening

Dinner in another time zone

The executive jet terminal is eight minutes from the gate, with customs clearance arranged on arrival and the car brought to the apron. Leave after dinner; be somewhere else for breakfast.
