Purchase details

Fixtures, fittings and integrated smart-home systems transfer with the residence. Selected furniture, art and the wine collection are available by separate negotiation. Full inventory is provided under NDA.
All enquiries are handled under strict confidentiality. Proof of funds is required before a private viewing is confirmed. Off-market and pre-launch residences are released only under a signed NDA.
The residence is offered freehold. An LLC or trust structure can be arranged through our private office, with introductions to tax and legal counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.
Viewings are strictly by appointment, arranged through the private client desk. Allow ninety minutes for a full walkthrough, and longer if you wish to review the architectural drawings with the design team.
