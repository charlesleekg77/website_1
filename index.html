Architectural Philosophy

Materials that age with grace

We represent houses built to be inherited, not replaced. Every residence in the collection is assessed on three measures: the honesty of its materials, the quality of its natural light, and its restraint.

01

Sustainable Materials

Locally quarried stone, FSC-certified timber and low-carbon concrete, specified for a fifty-year horizon.

02

Natural Lighting

Orientation, ceiling height and glazing are tuned to the sun path, so rooms need no artificial light by day.

03

Interior Finishes

Hand-troweled plaster, honed limestone and unlacquered brass — surfaces designed to patinate beautifully.
